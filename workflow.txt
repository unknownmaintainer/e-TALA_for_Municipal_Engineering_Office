1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM OVERVIEW
====================================================================================================
The Engineering Records Archiving and Retrieval Management System (eTala) is an internal,
web-based enterprise management and digital archiving system developed specifically for the
Municipal Engineering Office of the Local Government Unit (LGU) of Carigara, Leyte.

It eliminates manual, paper-heavy physical filing and centralizes all engineering data:
  1. Infrastructure Projects: Municipal-funded and Barangay-level public civil works.
  2. Official Engineering Permits: Building, Electrical, Mechanical, Occupancy, Fencing,
     Demolition, Renovation, and Sanitary permits.
  3. Digital Compliance Checklists: Document requirements loaded automatically per permit type.
  4. Geospatial Intelligence: Interactive GIS map locating projects across all 49 barangays.
  5. Executive Reporting: Instant, print-ready PDF and Excel reports for the Mayor, Sangguniang
     Bayan (SB), and Commission on Audit (COA).

2.2 TABLE-BY-TABLE DATA DICTIONARY & KEY FIELDS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. `CustomUser` (User Accounts & Authentication)
   • `id` (Primary Key): Auto-incrementing unique user identifier.
   • `username`: Auto-generated unique alphanumeric identifier (e.g. `maria.santos`).
   • `email`: Unique email address used for login and security verification.
   • `password`: PBKDF2 SHA256 hashed password string.
   • `full_name`: Official employee name (e.g. `Engr. Maria Santos`).
   • `designation`: Official job title (e.g. `Municipal Engineer`, `Building Inspector`).
   • `role`: Access level -> `admin` (Full Control) or `staff` (Standard Encoder).
   • `profile_picture`: Secure file path to user's uploaded avatar image.
   • `is_active`: Boolean flag indicating if account is active or deactivated.

2. `Barangay` (Geographical Master List)
   • `barangay_id` (Primary Key): Unique numeric ID (1 to 49).
   • `barangay_name`: Unique official name of the barangay.
   • `psgc_code`: Official PSA Philippine Standard Geographic Code (e.g. `0803715001`).
   • `district`: Poblacion, Coastal, Upland, or Rural district cluster.
   • `latitude` / `longitude`: Exact geocoded coordinates for GIS mapping.

3. `EngineeringRecord` (Central Master Records Table)
   • `record_id` (Primary Key): System-wide unique record number.
   • `record_type`: Primary category -> `Permit` or `Project`.
   • `project_scope`: Scope tier -> `Municipal` or `Barangay` (for projects).
   • `title`: Official title of project or applicant record.
   • `barangay_id`: Foreign Key pointing to `Barangay`.
   • `year`: Calendar/backlog year of the record.
   • `status`: Lifecycle state -> `pending`, `active`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `archived`.
   • `is_illegal_construction`: Boolean flag for unpermitted structures.
   • `illegal_compliance_status`: `unresolved`, `pending_permit`, or `resolved`.
   • `created_by_id`: Foreign Key pointing to `CustomUser` encoder.
   • `created_at` / `updated_at`: Precise timestamps.
   • `deleted_at`: Soft-delete timestamp used for 30-day trash retention calculation.

4. `PermitDetail` (Specialized Permit Attributes - 1:1 with EngineeringRecord)
   • `engineering_record_id`: Foreign Key (One-to-One) to `EngineeringRecord`.
   • `permit_type`: `Building`, `Electrical`, `Mechanical`, `Occupancy`, `Fencing`, `Demolition`.
   • `permit_number`: Official issued permit serial number (e.g. `2026-06-00001`).
   • `applicant_name`: Full legal name of property owner / applicant.
   • `building_type`: `Residential`, `Commercial`, `Industrial`, `Institutional`, `Agricultural`.
   • `date_issued`: Date the permit was officially granted.
   • `resolution_required`: Boolean flag if Sangguniang Bayan resolution was required.
   • `remarks`: Technical notes, inspection observations, or conditional clauses.

5. `ProjectDetail` (Specialized Infrastructure Attributes - 1:1 with EngineeringRecord)
   • `engineering_record_id`: Foreign Key (One-to-One) to `EngineeringRecord`.
   • `project_type`: `Roads and Bridges`, `Vertical Structures`, `Flood Control and Drainage`, `Potable Water`.
   • `funding_source`: `20% Development Fund`, `LGU General Fund`, `LGSF`, `National Fund`, etc.
   • `contractor`: Name of executing construction firm or "By Administration".
   • `project_cost`: Exact financial budget in Philippine Pesos (₱).
   • `project_status`: Engineering state (Planning, Procurement, Ongoing, Completed, Suspended).

6. `RequirementTemplate` (Checklist Rule Engine)
   • `template_id`: Unique ID of the checklist profile.
   • `record_type`: Target category (`Permit` or `Project`).
   • `subtype`: Specific type (e.g. `Building`, `Road & Bridge`, `Fencing`).
   • `scope`: Scope tier (`Municipal`, `Barangay`, or blank for permits).

7. `RequirementItem` (Individual Document Slot Definition)
   • `item_id`: Unique checklist item identifier.
   • `template_id`: Foreign Key to `RequirementTemplate`.
   • `parent_id`: Self-referencing Foreign Key for nested sub-documents (e.g. Architectural Plan under Building Plans).
   • `is_group`: Boolean indicating if this is a parent group container.
   • `name`: Document name (e.g. `Barangay Clearance`, `Fire Safety Inspection Certificate`).
   • `is_required`: Boolean flag indicating if mandatory for 100% compliance.

8. `RecordRequirement` (Per-Record Checklist Fulfillment Matrix)
   • `record_id`: Foreign Key to `EngineeringRecord`.
   • `requirement_item_id`: Foreign Key to `RequirementItem`.
   • `document_id`: Foreign Key (One-to-One) to uploaded `Document` file.
   • `is_fulfilled`: Boolean flag (True when valid file is attached).
   • `is_waived`: Boolean flag for legally exempt requirements.
   • `fulfilled_at` / `fulfilled_by`: Timestamp and user who satisfied the requirement.

9. `Document` (Uploaded File Metadata & Storage Registry)
   • `document_id`: Unique document file ID.
   • `engineering_record_id`: Foreign Key to `EngineeringRecord`.
   • `file_name`: Sanitized original filename.
   • `file`: Secure cloud/disk storage path.
   • `file_size`: File size in bytes (max 10 MB).
   • `version`: Version number (increments on document replacement).
   • `expiry_date`: Expiration date for time-sensitive clearances (e.g. FSIC).
   • `uploaded_by_id`: Foreign Key to `CustomUser`.

10. `AuditLog` (System-Wide Accountability Log)
    • `log_id`: Unique log sequence ID.
    • `user_id`: Foreign Key to `CustomUser` who performed the action.
    • `action`: Short, clear description of the operation (e.g. `Created Building Permit: 2026-06-00001`).
    • `target_record_id`: ID of affected engineering record.
    • `ip_address`: Client IP address of encoder.
    • `performed_at`: Precise immutable timestamp.

11. `UserDevice` (Device Fingerprinting & Security Trust Registry)
    • `device_token`: Secure cryptographic hash representing browser/device hardware signature.
    • `user_id`: Foreign Key to `CustomUser`.
    • `device_name`: Human-readable name (e.g. `Windows 11 • Chrome 124`).
    • `status`: `approved`, `pending`, or `rejected`.
    • `last_seen_at`: Timestamp of latest access.

12. `LoginAttempt` (Brute-Force & Security Audit Log)
    • `email_attempted`: Email address entered during login.
    • `success`: Boolean (True if authentication succeeded).
    • `ip_address`: Request IP address.
    • `timestamp`: Exact time of login attempt.

13. `BlockedIP` (Security Firewall Blacklist)
    • `ip_address`: Blocked IP address.
    • `reason`: Reason for restriction (e.g. `Excessive brute-force password attacks`).
    • `blocked_at`: Timestamp of restriction.

2.3 FOREIGN KEY RULES & DATA INTEGRITY CONSTRAINTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To guarantee that records can NEVER be accidentally broken or orphaned:

• `on_delete=models.PROTECT`:
  - `Barangay` -> `EngineeringRecord`: Prevents deleting a Barangay if it contains any records.
  - `CustomUser` -> `EngineeringRecord`: Prevents deleting a User if they have authored records.
    (This is why former employees are Deactivated rather than Deleted).
  - `RequirementItem` -> `RecordRequirement`: Prevents deleting requirement definitions that are
    actively attached to existing records.

• `on_delete=models.CASCADE`:
  - `EngineeringRecord` -> `PermitDetail` / `ProjectDetail` / `Document` / `RecordRequirement`:
    When a record is permanently purged after 30 days in Trash, all its attached details and
    document records are cleanly and completely purged together.


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4 DATABASE INDEXING STRATEGY (SUB-300ms PERFORMANCE)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
High-speed database indexes are configured on frequently queried columns:
  • Single Indexes: `record_type`, `project_scope`, `barangay`, `status`, `year`, `title`, `deleted_at`.
  • Composite Indexes:
    - `[record_type, status]`: High-speed filtering of active permits vs projects.
    - `[barangay, year]`: Fast reporting aggregations across the 49 barangays.
    - `[status, deleted_at]`: Instant lookup of trash records expiring within 30 days.

3. END-TO-END SECURITY ARCHITECTURE & PROCESS FLOWS
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 AUTHENTICATION & PASSWORD SECURITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Algorithm: Passwords are encrypted using Django's PBKDF2 (Password-Based Key Derivation Function 2)
  with a SHA-256 hash and 600,000 cryptographic iterations. Plaintext passwords are NEVER stored.
• Password Complexity Rule: Must be at least 8 characters long, containing at least one uppercase
  letter, one lowercase letter, and one number.
• Password History Prevention: The system retains password hashes in `PasswordHistory` to prevent
  users from immediately reusing previous passwords.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 5-ATTEMPT LOCKOUT & 15-MINUTE AUTOMATIC COOL-OFF PROCESS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Failure Counter: Every failed password attempt for an account is tracked in real-time.
• Lockout Threshold: On the 5th consecutive failure, `django-axes` triggers an immediate lockout.
• 15-Minute Auto Cool-Off: The lockout remains active for exactly 15 minutes.
• Automatic Recovery: The user simply waits 15 minutes. After 15 minutes, the timer expires and the
  login form unlocks automatically without requiring administrative intervention.
• Counter Reset on Success: Entering the correct password before reaching 5 attempts resets the
  failure count back to zero (0).
• Administrator Override: An Administrator can unlock any account immediately by resetting the user's
  password or executing `python manage.py axes_reset`.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 MULTI-SESSION & DEVICE SECURITY VERIFICATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Multi-Session Flexibility: Staff members can keep active sessions open on their desktop workstation
  and their laptop/tablet simultaneously without being forcefully disconnected.
• Device Fingerprinting (`UserDevice`): Each device generates a unique cryptographic device token based
  on hardware characteristics and browser headers.
• New Device Security Notification: Signing in from an unrecognized device triggers an instant email
  alert to the user to confirm the login was authentic.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) & VIEW PROTECTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Server-Side Authorization: Every web route is strictly decorated with `@login_required`,
  `@admin_required`, or `@staff_or_admin_required`.
• Non-Admin Lockdown: Engineering Staff who attempt to access `/users/`, `/settings/`, or system backup
  endpoints are immediately blocked with an `HTTP 403 Forbidden` response.
• Financial Masking: Engineering Staff see masked project costs (e.g. `₱ ••••••••`) for records
  belonging to other departments to preserve municipal financial privacy.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 CSRF, XSS SANITIZATION & INPUT DEFENSE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• CSRF Protection: Every POST/PUT/DELETE request requires a valid, cryptographically signed
  Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) token cookie. Requests lacking this token are rejected.
• XSS Defense: All user text inputs pass through `sanitize_input()` which strips dangerous HTML tags,
  JavaScript execution payloads (`<script>`, `onerror=`), and control characters.
• Search Sanitization: Universal search inputs allow only alphanumeric text, spaces, and safe
  punctuation (`-_.#()',/`). Unsafe script symbols are removed in real-time before querying.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 TIME-LIMITED SIGNED TOKEN DOCUMENT SECURITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Private Storage: Attached documents (PDFs, structural plans, tax scans) are stored in secure
  cloud buckets and are NOT directly accessible via public URLs.
• 10-Minute Ephemeral Tokens: When an authorized user clicks to preview a document, the server
  generates a time-limited signed URL that expires in 10 minutes. Direct links cannot be shared or leaked.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.7 IP FIREWALL & AUTOMATED BLOCKING MECHANISM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• `IPBlockMiddleware`: Inspects the client IP address on every incoming web request.
• Instant 403 Blocking: If an IP address matches an entry in `BlockedIP`, the request is immediately
  terminated with an access-denied screen before reaching the database or application logic.

4. DEEP OPERATIONAL PROCESS FLOWS (STEP-BY-STEP)
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 1: USER SIGN-IN, SESSION MANAGEMENT & PASSWORD RECOVERY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Step 1: User visits `/login/` and enters their registered Email and Password.
2. Step 2: Server checks credentials. If valid -> logs in, creates a session, and redirects to Dashboard.
3. Step 3: If invalid -> increments failure counter. If counter reaches 5 -> displays 15-minute cool-off message.
4. Step 4 (Forgot Password): User clicks "Forgot Password?", enters email, and receives a secure,
   signed one-time reset link valid for 24 hours. Clicking the link allows creating a new password.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 2: EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD & LIVE VISUAL INTELLIGENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Summary KPIs: Live counts of Total Records, Municipal Projects, Barangay Projects, Permits,
   Incomplete Checklists, and Trash items.
2. Real-Time Charts:
   • Annual Encoding Volume (Chart.js Bar Chart): Visualizes infrastructure growth across years.
   • Permit Sub-Type Distribution (Chart.js Doughnut Chart): Shows proportion of Building vs
     Electrical vs Occupancy permits.
3. Quick Navigation: Direct shortcut buttons to create records, inspect reports, or view GIS map.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 3: THE 3-STEP GUIDED RECORD ENCODING WIZARD
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• STEP 1: SCOPE & CATEGORIZATION (`/records/create/step1/`)
  1. User selects Category: Municipal Project, Barangay Project, or Engineering Permit.
  2. User selects Sub-type (e.g. Building, Electrical, Roads & Bridges, Flood Control).
  3. User picks Barangay from the 49 official barangays of Carigara.
  4. User enters Calendar Year (validated between 1990 and current year).

• STEP 2: TECHNICAL & FINANCIAL DETAILS (`/records/create/step2/`)
  1. For Projects: Title, Contractor, Project Cost (₱), Funding Source, Status, Dates.
  2. For Permits: Permit Number (`2026-06-00001`), Applicant Name, Building Classification, Dates.
  3. Form validation triggers: Ensures mandatory fields are present and valid.

• STEP 3: DIGITAL CHECKLIST GENERATION (`/records/create/step3/`)
  1. The system matches the `RequirementTemplate` and automatically creates checklist slots.
  2. Encoder can upload required files (PDF, JPG, DOCX) immediately or save and upload later.
  3. Record is saved with status `active` and assigned a permanent unique `record_id`.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 4: AUTOMATED DOCUMENT CHECKLISTS, VERSIONING & IN-BROWSER PREVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Dedicated Slots: Each requirement (e.g. Structural Analysis, FSIC) displays as an upload slot.
2. Direct Upload: Drag and drop or browse files up to 10 MB.
3. Progress Percentage: A completion pill dynamically computes:
     Completion Rate = (Fulfilled Required Documents / Total Required Documents) * 100%
4. Document Replacement (Versioning): Uploading a new scan over an existing slot replaces the active
   file, increments `version` (e.g. v1 -> v2), and updates the fulfillment timestamp.
5. In-Browser Viewer: Clicking "Preview" opens an inline modal displaying the PDF or image with
   pan, zoom, and rotate controls without downloading to local disk.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 5: DOCUMENT EXPIRY TRACKING & PROACTIVE NOTIFICATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Expiry Recording: Documents like Fire Safety Clearance or Insurance record an `expiry_date`.
2. 30-Day Window Alert: When a document is within 30 days of expiring, the system generates an
   alert in the Topbar Bell Notifications.
3. Persistent Read State: Clicking or marking the notification as read permanently records
   `NOTIF_READ:<doc_id>` in the database. The alert will NEVER reappear as unread.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 6: UNIVERSAL 250px LIVE SEARCH & MULTI-TOKEN QUERY ENGINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Standard Compact Search Pill: Standardized width of 250px across all modules.
2. 300ms Debounce: As user types, a 300ms timer waits before sending an asynchronous AJAX request.
3. Multi-Token Query: Query "2026 Canfabi Building" matches records with Year 2026, Barangay Canfabi,
   and Permit Type Building simultaneously.
4. Keyboard Shortcut: Pressing `Esc` clears the search instantly.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 7: TRASH LIFECYCLE (SOFT-DELETE -> 30-DAY RECOVERY -> AUTO-PURGE)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Soft-Delete: Admin deletes a record -> Status changes to `archived` and `deleted_at` is set to now.
2. 30-Day Safety Window: The record sits in `/archive/`. Details and files remain completely safe.
3. 1-Click Restore: Admin clicks "Restore" in kebab menu (⋮) -> Status returns to `active`.
4. 30-Day Auto-Purge: When `deleted_at` exceeds 30 days, `purge_expired_trash_records` permanently
   erases the record and deletes all associated files from cloud storage.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 8: 49 BARANGAYS MASTER GRID & LEAFLET GIS MAPPING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Master Grid: 49 cards showing summary projects, permits, and accomplishments per barangay.
2. GIS Map Engine: Interactive Leaflet map centered at `[11.2608, 124.6755]` (Carigara Town Center).
3. Barangay Workspace: Dedicated view for any barangay listing all historical civil works and permits.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 9: ILLEGAL CONSTRUCTION FLAGGING & REGULARIZATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Flagging Violation: Staff flags unpermitted work, attaches inspection photos and location.
2. Tracking Pipeline: `Unresolved` -> `Permit Application Filed` -> `Complied / Permitted`.
3. Report Separation Rule: Violations are monitored strictly as enforcement indicators and are NEVER
   counted as positive municipal accomplishments in official reports.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 10: OFFICIAL ACCOMPLISHMENT REPORTS & DUAL PDF/EXCEL EXPORT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 49-Barangay Master Matrix: Computes total projects and permits filtered by Year, Month, or Quarter.
2. Standard Formula: Total Accomplishments = Infrastructure Projects + Engineering Permits.
3. Dual Export Formats:
   • Print-Ready PDF: Formatted Legal/A4 layout, centered official LGU letterhead, signature blocks.
   • Excel Spreadsheet (.xlsx): Formatted data matrix for SB and COA auditing.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 11: USER MANAGEMENT & SAFE EMPLOYEE DEACTIVATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Account Creation: Admin creates staff account (Name, Email, Role, Temporary Password).
2. Auto-Username: Clean username generated automatically from email prefix.
3. Deactivation vs Deletion: Inactive staff accounts are Deactivated (`is_active=False`). Historical
   records authored by the staff remain 100% intact in the database.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 12: AUDIT LOGGING & DATABASE ANTI-BLOAT ARCHITECTURE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Accountability Logging: Records Create, Edit, Trash, Restore, Purge, and User Role changes.
2. Anti-Bloat Filter: Routine operations (viewing pages, downloading files, login attempts) are
   ignored to prevent database bloat.
3. CSV Export: Admin can export the complete audit trail to CSV anytime.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESS 13: AUTOMATED DAILY BACKUPS, 14-DAY RETENTION & DISASTER RECOVERY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Daily Snapshots: System takes an automated JSON snapshot of all database records every 24 hours.
2. 14-Day Retention: Retains the 14 latest backup files and auto-deletes older snapshots.
3. 1-Click Manual Backup: Admin can download a complete system backup archive anytime.
4. Disaster Recovery: Admin can upload a backup file to restore the entire database in minutes.


====================================================================================================
5. BUSINESS LOGIC, MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS & CALCULATION ENGINES
====================================================================================================

1. OFFICIAL ACCOMPLISHMENT FORMULA:
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   TOTAL ACCOMPLISHMENTS = TOTAL INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS + TOTAL OFFICIAL PERMITS ISSUED
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   * Violations and illegal constructions are strictly excluded from positive accomplishment totals.

2. DOCUMENT CHECKLIST COMPLETION RATE:
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   COMPLETION RATE (%) = (FULFILLED REQUIRED DOCUMENTS / TOTAL REQUIRED DOCUMENTS) * 100%
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   * Parent group headers are excluded; only leaf requirements determine the mathematical percentage.

3. TRASH REMAINING DAYS FORMULA:
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   DAYS REMAINING = MAX(0, 30 - (CURRENT_TIMESTAMP - DELETED_AT_TIMESTAMP) in Days)
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   * Auto-purge triggers when DAYS REMAINING reaches 0.

7. COMPREHENSIVE ZIP & EXPORT ARCHIVAL DATA STRUCTURES
====================================================================================================

eTala provides four (4) standardized ZIP export hierarchies designed for municipal engineering recordkeeping, COA audit compliance, offline document archiving, and disaster recovery:

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SINGLE RECORD ZIP ARCHIVE (Export Record ZIP)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Triggered Via: Record Details page (`/records/<record_id>/download-zip/`)
• Target File  : `<Sanitized_Record_Title>_Documents.zip`
• Folder Hierarchy & Contents:
  ├── 00_RECORD_SUMMARY.txt             <-- Formatted plaintext metadata sheet (Title, Date, Cost, Owner)
  ├── 01_Building_Plans/                <-- Parent requirement group folder (prefixed with 2-digit order)
  │   ├── Architectural_Plans.pdf
  │   └── Structural_Calculations.pdf
  ├── 02_Program_of_Works.pdf           <-- Direct root file for standalone single leaf requirements
  ├── 03_Detailed_Estimates.pdf
  ├── 04_Specifications.pdf
  └── Additional_Attachments/           <-- Site inspection photos, incident notes & auxiliary files
      ├── Site_Inspection_Photo_1.jpg
      └── Topographic_Map.pdf

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. REQUIREMENT CATEGORY / SLOT ZIP ARCHIVE (Export Category ZIP)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. COMPREHENSIVE CLIENT FAQ & TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE (50 ESSENTIAL QUESTIONS)
====================================================================================================

PART I: USER ACCOUNTS, LOGIN, SESSIONS & SECURITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q01: What happens if an employee types the wrong password 5 times in a row?
A01: The account is temporarily locked for safety. You have three easy ways to handle it:
     • Option A (Standard): Wait 15 minutes. The timer will automatically unlock the login screen.
     • Option B (Immediate): Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen to reset your password via email.
     • Option C (Admin): An Administrator can reset the password immediately from the User Management page.

Q02: What happens if I type the wrong password 3 times, but get it right on the 4th try?
A02: The failed attempt counter immediately resets back to zero (0). You will not be locked out.

Q03: Can multiple engineers or staff use the same account on different computers at the same time?
A03: Yes! eTala supports flexible multi-session logins so staff can work on desktop PCs and laptops
     simultaneously without being abruptly disconnected.

Q04: How do I change or recover my password if I forget it?
A04: Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page, enter your registered email address, and click
     "Send Reset Link". Open the email in your inbox and click the secure link to set your new password.

Q05: What are the minimum password strength rules for eTala?
A05: Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and contain at least one uppercase letter (A-Z), one
     lowercase letter (a-z), and at least one number (0-9).

Q06: Can I reuse my old password when changing it?
A06: No. For municipal security compliance, eTala checks your password history to prevent reusing recent passwords.

Q07: What happens when an employee resigns, transfers, or retires from the Engineering Office?
A07: The Administrator goes to User Management (`/users/`) and toggles the user's status to "Deactivated".
     This prevents future logins while permanently protecting all historical records and audit logs created
     by that employee (zero broken data).

Q08: Why did I receive an email saying "New Device Login Alert"?
A08: Whenever your account signs in from a computer, phone, or browser that you have not used before,
     eTala automatically notifies you by email so you know your account is safe.

Q09: How can I update my profile name, designation, or avatar picture?
A09: Click your name or avatar in the upper-right corner of the topbar, select "User Profile", make your
     desired changes, and click "Save Changes".

Q10: What should I do when leaving a shared office workstation?
A10: Always click your avatar on the top right and select "Log out" to securely terminate your session.


PART II: RECORD ENCODING, PERMITS & PROJECT MANAGEMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q11: Can I save a new record even if I don't have all the required document attachments yet?
A11: Yes! In Step 3 of the Creation Wizard, you can save the record as Active. You can return anytime to
     upload the remaining documents as applicants or contractors submit them.

Q12: What is the difference between a Municipal Project and a Barangay Project?
A12: Municipal Projects are major public infrastructure funded directly by the Municipal Government (e.g.
     20% Development Fund, Municipal Hall facilities). Barangay Projects are local community civil works
     funded through Barangay Funds or specific local community allocations.

Q13: What is the standard format for entering a Permit Number?
A13: Use the official municipal standard format (e.g. `2026-06-00001` or standard sequence numbers like `4333333`).

Q14: Can we encode backlog records from previous years (e.g. 2020, 2018)?
A14: Yes! In Step 1 of the Creation Wizard, you can select any valid past year to digitize historical physical records.

Q15: What should I do if I made a mistake in the Title, Barangay, or Project Cost?
A15: Open the record's detail page, click the "Edit Record" button at the top-right, modify the information,
     and click "Save Changes". The edit will be logged automatically in the system audit trail.

Q16: Why are project costs masked (e.g. ₱ ••••••••) for some users?
A16: To protect municipal financial privacy, non-admin staff see masked project costs for records authored by
     other departments unless granted administrative permission.

Q17: What does the "Sangguniang Bayan (SB) Resolution Required" checkbox mean?
A17: It indicates that the project or permit required a formal municipal council legislative authorization
     or endorsement before issuance.

Q18: What project statuses are available in eTala?
A18: Projects can be set to: Planning, Procurement, Ongoing, Completed, or Suspended.

Q19: Can an Engineering Staff member delete a record?
A19: No. Only Administrators have permission to move records to the Trash to prevent accidental data loss.

Q20: How are multi-phase construction projects recorded?
A20: Encode each phase clearly in the title (e.g. "Concreting of Local Access Road - Phase 1" and
     "Concreting of Local Access Road - Phase 2") so each phase tracks its own budget and checklist.


PART III: DOCUMENT CHECKLISTS, UPLOADS & IN-BROWSER PREVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q21: What file formats are accepted for document uploads?
A21: eTala accepts PDF documents (`.pdf`), high-resolution images (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`), and Word documents (`.docx`).

Q22: What is the maximum allowed file size for each document?
A22: Each individual document slot accepts files up to 10.0 Megabytes (MB).

Q23: Do I need to download a PDF or blueprint to my computer to view it?
A23: No! eTala features a built-in In-Browser Document Viewer. Clicking "Preview" opens the document
     instantly inside the system with zoom, rotate, and multi-page reading tools.

Q24: What happens if I upload the wrong document or need to replace a blurry scan?
A24: Click the "Replace" button on that document slot and choose the new file. The system automatically
     updates the slot to the new file and increments the version counter (e.g. v1 -> v2).

Q25: What does the checklist percentage badge (e.g. 80%) mean?
A25: It indicates the proportion of mandatory required documents that have been uploaded. When all required
     documents are attached, the badge turns bright green (100% Complete).

Q26: Can a required document be "Waived" if not applicable to a specific applicant?
A26: Yes. Authorized staff can click "Waive" on a requirement slot for legally exempt cases, satisfying the slot.

Q27: How does Document Expiry Tracking work?
A27: When uploading time-sensitive documents (e.g. Fire Safety Inspection Certificates or Contractor Licenses),
     you can set an Expiry Date. The system monitors this date and alerts you 30 days before it expires.

Q28: Are uploaded municipal documents accessible to the public on the internet?
A28: No. Documents are stored in secure cloud storage and can ONLY be viewed through time-limited signed
     tokens (10-minute expiry) accessible strictly to logged-in authorized staff.

Q29: What happens if our office internet is slow during an upload?
A29: eTala uses asynchronous streaming uploads with progress indicators so you can see the upload progress.

Q30: Can we upload multiple building plan pages as one file?
A30: Yes! We recommend scanning multi-page architectural, structural, or sanitary plans into a single multi-page PDF.


PART IV: SEARCH, FILTERING & RECORDS NAVIGATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q31: How fast is the search in eTala?
A31: eTala searches across thousands of municipal records in under 300 milliseconds using instant live AJAX debouncing.

Q32: What can I type in the search bar to find a record?
A32: You can search by Record Title, Applicant/Owner Name, Contractor Name, Permit Number, Barangay Name,
     Year, or Category (e.g. typing "2026 Canfabi Solar" finds matching solar records in Canfabi for 2026).

Q33: How do I quickly clear what I typed in the search bar?
A33: You can click the `(X)` icon inside the search bar or simply press the `Escape` (Esc) key on your keyboard.

Q34: What characters are allowed in the search bar?
A34: Letters (`a-z`, `A-Z`), Numbers (`0-9`), Spaces, and standard search punctuation (`- _ . # ( ) ' , /`).
     Deliberate programming symbols (`< > { } " ; \ ^ $`) are stripped automatically for security.

Q35: How do I view records from only one specific barangay?
A35: Go to "Records", click the "Barangay" dropdown filter, and choose your desired barangay from the 49 options.

Q36: Can I export the exact search results I am viewing on screen?
A36: Yes! After searching or filtering, clicking "Export" downloads an Excel or PDF containing strictly the filtered results.

Q37: Where can I find records that were encoded years ago?
A37: Go to "Records" and choose "All Records", or select the specific historical year from the Year filter dropdown.


PART V: TRASH, ARCHIVING & 30-DAY AUTO-PURGE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q38: What happens when an Administrator deletes an active record?
A38: The record is NOT permanently erased. It is safely moved to the "Trash & Archive" area (Soft Delete).

Q39: How long does an accidentally deleted record stay in the Trash?
A39: It stays safely in the Trash for exactly 30 calendar days.

Q40: How do I restore a record that was deleted by mistake?
A40: An Administrator opens "Trash & Archive" in the sidebar, finds the record, clicks the kebab menu (⋮),
     and selects "Restore". The record and all its attached files return to the active list immediately.

Q41: What happens after 30 days in the Trash?
A41: The system automatically purges the record and permanently deletes its attached files from the server
     disk to free up space (30-Day Auto-Purge Policy).

Q42: Can a record be restored after the 30-day auto-purge has passed?
A42: No. Once 30 days pass and the record is purged, it cannot be restored unless retrieved from a system backup file.

Q43: Why did a Trash Warning notification stop showing after I clicked it?
A43: eTala uses permanent notification IDs. Once you read or click a warning, it stays marked as read permanently.


PART VI: 49 BARANGAYS & LEAFLET GIS MAPPING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q44: Are all 49 barangays of Carigara included in the system?
A44: Yes! All 49 barangays (Poblacion Barangays 1–5, coastal, upland, and rural barangays) are fully integrated.

Q45: How do I view project locations on the interactive map?
A45: Go to "Barangays" in the sidebar and click the "Show Map" button. The interactive Leaflet GIS map
     will open showing all barangay project markers centered on Carigara town center (`11.2608, 124.6755`).

Q46: What happens when I click a marker on the map?
A46: A popup card appears showing the Barangay name, total completed projects, and total issued permits with a
     link to open that barangay's dedicated workspace.


PART VII: ILLEGAL CONSTRUCTIONS & VIOLATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q47: How do we record an unpermitted or illegal construction activity?
A47: Click "New Record", choose "Illegal Construction Violation", input the location, owner details, and attach
     inspection photos.

Q48: Are illegal construction violations counted in official municipal accomplishment totals?
A48: No! Violations are tracked strictly as Enforcement Indicators and are excluded from positive accomplishment
     metrics so official LGU reports remain accurate and compliant.


PART VIII: REPORTS, AUDIT LOGS & SYSTEM BACKUPS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q49: How do I generate and print official reports for the Mayor, Sangguniang Bayan, or COA?
A49: Go to "Reports" in the sidebar, choose your desired Year, Quarter, or Month, and click "Export to PDF".
     The system produces a document with official Carigara LGU letterheads and signature blocks ready for signing.

Q50: How do system backups work and how often are they performed?
A50: eTala automatically creates a complete database backup snapshot every 24 hours. The system keeps the 14 latest
     daily backups and cleans older snapshots automatically. Administrators can also click "Backup Database"
     anytime to download an immediate backup copy to their computer.
