1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES
====================================================================================================
eTala is the centralized digital archiving and records management system built specifically for 
the Municipal Engineering Office of Carigara, Leyte.

Main Objectives:
1. Paperless & Organized Archiving: Eliminate misplaced blueprints, permits, and paper forms.
2. Fast Record Retrieval: Find any permit, applicant, or project in less than 3 seconds.
3. 49 Barangays Monitoring: Track all civil works and permits across every barangay in Carigara.
4. Transparency & Audit Compliance: Generate official accomplishment reports ready for Sangguniang
   Bayan (SB) and Commission on Audit (COA) submissions.
5. Data Safety & Protection: Keep official engineering records permanently safe from fire, flood,
   or accidental loss through automated daily cloud backups.


====================================================================================================
2. KEY FEATURES & CAPABILITIES
====================================================================================================

1. GUIDED RECORD ENCODING (PERMITS & INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS)
   • 3-Step simple form for adding Building Permits, Electrical Permits, Fencing Permits, Demolition
     Permits, and Municipal/Barangay Infrastructure Projects.
   • Automatically links each record to its respective Barangay, Applicant/Owner, Contractor, and Budget.

2. DYNAMIC DOCUMENT CHECKLISTS
   • The system automatically determines which supporting documents are required depending on the
     type of permit or project (e.g., Architectural Plans, Structural Calculations, Sanitary Plans,
     Electrical Plans, Barangay Clearance, Tax Declaration, Zoning Clearance).
   • Shows a visual progress bar indicating checklist completion (e.g., 75% complete).

3. 49 BARANGAYS MASTER GRID & GIS MAP
   • A dedicated page for every barangay in Carigara showing its total accomplishments and active works.
   • Interactive municipal map showing exact locations and project densities per barangay.

4. OFFICIAL REPORTS & 1-CLICK DUAL EXPORT
   • Generates monthly, quarterly, and annual accomplishment summary reports across all 49 barangays.
   • Dual Export:
     - Printable Official PDF: Clean, centered LGU Carigara letterhead, ready for signing.
     - Excel Spreadsheet (.xlsx): Formatted data table for auditing and analysis.

5. VIOLATIONS & ILLEGAL CONSTRUCTION ENFORCEMENT
   • Dedicated monitoring for unpermitted structures and building code violations.
   • Tracks case status from "Under Investigation" until "Complied / Permit Issued".
   • Automatically separated from positive accomplishment reports to maintain clean audit records.

6. UNIVERSAL LIVE SEARCH
   • Fast search bar accessible on every page (or press Ctrl + K).
   • Instantly search by Applicant Name, Permit Number (#2026-...), Project Title, Contractor, or Barangay.

7. TRASH WITH 30-DAY RECOVERY SAFETY NET
   • Deleted records are never permanently erased immediately.
   • Stays safely in the Trash bin for 30 days where authorized staff can restore them in 1 click.
   • After 30 days, expired trash is cleanly purged to save storage space.

8. UNIFIED DAILY BACKUP & RESTORATION
   • Automatic Scheduled Backup runs every midnight (12:00 AM) to secure all database records and files.
   • On-Demand 1-Click Backup: Admin can download a complete ZIP file archive anytime.
   • A single "Last Backup" status badge clearly displays the most recent backup activity.

9. AUDIT LOGS & USER ACCOUNTABILITY
   • Every important action (creating records, editing details, uploading files, changing statuses)
     is automatically logged with the user's name, date, and exact timestamp.

4. END-TO-END SYSTEM WORKFLOW (STEP-BY-STEP)
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: SIGN-IN & TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (2FA)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Staff or Engineer enters their Email (or Username) and Password on the Login page.
2. If logging in from a new/unrecognized device, eTala sends a secure 6-digit verification code (OTP)
   directly to the user's registered Gmail address.
3. The user enters the 6-digit code to verify their identity and securely access the system.
4. Trusted devices are remembered to enable fast, seamless future logins.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: DASHBOARD & REAL-TIME OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Upon logging in, the Executive Dashboard presents real-time numbers:
   • Total Permits Issued vs Total Infrastructure Projects
   • Digitization Compliance Percentage
   • Documents Expiring Soon alerts
   • Recent Office Activity Feed
2. Quick Action buttons allow 1-click creation of new records or viewing urgent items.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: ENCODING A NEW PERMIT OR INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click the "+ Add Record" or "+ New Permit / Project" button.
2. Complete the 3-Step Guided Encoding:
   • Step 1 (Basic Info): Select Type (Permit or Infrastructure Project), Specific Subtype (Building,
     Electrical, Road, Bridge, etc.), and Barangay location.
   • Step 2 (Entity Details): Enter Applicant/Owner name, Permit Number, Contractor, Budget, and Dates.
   • Step 3 (Review & Confirm): Summary review before saving into the database.
3. Once saved, a green confirmation toast notification confirms the record is active.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: UPLOADING REQUIREMENTS & DOCUMENT CHECKLISTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the Record Details page.
2. The system presents the required checklist items (e.g., Architectural Plans, Structural Sheet).
3. Click "Upload File" on any item:
   • Choose a PDF file or photo (max 50MB per file).
   • You can upload single files or batch upload multiple attachments at once.
4. The checklist progress bar automatically updates as documents are fulfilled.
5. All uploaded documents can be previewed directly in the browser without downloading.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: MANAGING 49 BARANGAYS & GIS MAP
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Navigate to the "Barangays" section.
2. Browse through all 49 barangay summary cards or click any barangay name.
3. The Barangay Workspace lists all active and completed engineering works in that community.
4. The Interactive Map displays pins and density heatmaps showing where municipal developments are concentrated.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: TRACKING ILLEGAL CONSTRUCTIONS & VIOLATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. If an unauthorized structure is discovered during field inspection, click "Flag Violation".
2. Enter the Location, Property Owner (if known), and upload inspection photos/notices.
3. The case remains under the "Illegal Constructions" tab with an "Active Violation" status.
4. When the owner files for proper permits and settles requirements, click "Resolve Violation" to
   issue their official permit and mark the record as "Complied".

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7: GENERATING OFFICIAL ACCOMPLISHMENT REPORTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Navigate to the "Reports" section.
2. Filter accomplishments by Year, Quarter, or specific Month.
3. Click "Download PDF" to generate an official print-ready document formatted with the official
   Carigara Municipal Engineering Office header and signature blocks.
4. Click "Download Excel" for an editable spreadsheet for government audits (COA / SB).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8: UNIVERSAL QUICK SEARCH
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Use the search bar at the top or press `Ctrl + K`.
2. Type any keyword (e.g., "Juan", "Road", "2026-08", "Poblacion", "Mega Builder").
3. Results appear instantly with clear icon indicators for Permit Number, Contractor, and Owner.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9: TRASH & 30-DAY ACCIDENTAL DELETE RECOVERY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. If a record is deleted by mistake, it is moved to the "Trash" tab.
2. The record stays completely intact for 30 days.
3. To recover, simply click the three dots (⋮) on the row and select "Restore".
4. Records older than 30 days are automatically cleaned up to prevent clutter.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 10: UNIFIED DAILY BACKUPS & DISASTER RECOVERY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Automatic Backup: The server automatically creates a complete database snapshot every night at 12:00 AM.
2. Manual 1-Click Backup: Administrators can click "Download Backup (ZIP)" in Settings to save an
   instant copy to their computer or external hard drive.
3. Restoration: In case of hardware replacement, the admin can upload the backup file to restore all
   records and documents in minutes.

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
====================================================================================================

Q: What happens if an employee accidentally deletes an important permit?
A: No need to panic. The record is safely placed in the Trash bin. Any authorized staff or admin can
   click "Restore" within 30 days, and the permit along with all its uploaded files will return to active status.

Q: Can we use eTala on smartphones and tablets during field inspections?
A: Yes. eTala is fully mobile-responsive. Field staff can open the system on their phone or tablet to
   search records, inspect requirements, or take photos and upload them directly to a violation case.

Q: How do we generate the annual report for the Mayor and Sangguniang Bayan?
A: Go to Reports, select the desired year, and click "Download PDF" or "Download Excel". The system
   instantly compiles all accomplishments across all 49 barangays with complete totals and signature blocks.

Q: Where are the files stored and are they safe?
A: Files are securely stored in redundant cloud storage with enterprise encryption. Daily automated
   backups guarantee that data remains completely safe against server failure or disasters.
