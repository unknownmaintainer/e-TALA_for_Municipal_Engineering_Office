eTala Quick User Guide
1. Archiving New Records
  • 1
    Click + Add Record in the sidebar or browse page.
  • 2
    Choose category: Municipal, Barangay, or Permit.
  • 3
    Fill in project title, budget / permit number, and barangay.
  • 4
    Click Save Record to create the official archive entry.
2. Document Checklists
  • 1
    Open any record detail page to view its MEO checklist.
  • 2
    Click Upload on any requirement slot to attach PDF scans.
  • 3
    Use Batch Upload to drag-and-drop multiple files at once.
  • 4
    Click Download All (ZIP) to export the full file package.
3. GIS Satellite & Barangays
  • 1
    Click Barangays in the sidebar to open the interactive map.
  • 2
    Click on any of the 49 Barangays to inspect localized records.
  • 3
    Switch to Google Satellite view to inspect actual terrain.
  • 4
    Pin project sites and field observations directly on the map.
4. Shortcuts & Search
  • 1
    Press Ctrl + K to search records & barangays instantly.
  • 2
    Press ? or Shift + / to open this User Guide anytime.
  • 3
    Press Esc to quickly close any active modal or search popup.
  • 4
    Use the Sun / Moon toggle in the header for Dark Mode.
