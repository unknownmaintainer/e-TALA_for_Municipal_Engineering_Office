eTala

Municipal Engineering Office · LGU Carigara, Leyte
v1.0.0 · 2026

Digital archiving and records management system with integrated Barangay GIS mapping for the Municipal Engineering Office of Carigara, Leyte.

System Features

Permits & Projects Archiving

Digital encoding, document archiving, and compliance checklist tracking for municipal records.

Barangay Records & GIS Map

Organizing records per barangay with interactive GIS mapping for pinpointing project sites.

Illegal Construction Records

Logging unpermitted structures, site inspection photos, violation notices, and compliance status.

Reports & Activity Logs

Generating printable summary PDF reports and tracking user action logs for office auditing.

Engineering Staff
