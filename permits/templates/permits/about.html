eTala

Municipal Engineering Office · Carigara, Leyte

eTala is a web-based engineering records archiving and retrieval platform developed for the Municipal Engineering Office of Carigara, Leyte. It provides authorized personnel with a centralized system to manage structural permits, infrastructure projects, unpermitted developments, and audit logs.

Core System Capabilities

Digital Archiving

Digitally organize, index, and preserve municipal engineering records and uploaded attachments.

Permits & Infrastructure Projects

Manage building, electrical, and occupancy permits alongside municipal project records.

Smart Search & Filter

Locate records instantly by title, applicant name, permit number, or barangay location.

Illegal Construction Monitoring

Flag, track, and monitor unpermitted building developments for enforcement compliance.

Analytics & Summaries

Generate real-time permit status counts, municipal summaries, and exportable reports.

Audit Logging & Access Control

Track system transactions with role-based access for staff and administrators.

System Access & Permissions

Engineering Staff
Authorized to encode, search, inspect, and retrieve municipal engineering records.
Administrators
Full system access including user management, activity logs, and trash bin management.
