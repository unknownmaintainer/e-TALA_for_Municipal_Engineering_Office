Centralized digital records archive and GIS monitoring platform for municipal permits, infrastructure projects, and compliance enforcement across the 49 barangays of Carigara.

Core Modules

4 Essential Pillars

Digital Records Archive

Secure indexing, archiving, and retrieval of Building, Electrical, and Occupancy permits with documents.

GIS Workspaces

Interactive GIS mapping organizing municipal permits and projects across all 49 Carigara barangays.

Violation Tracking

Real-time flagging, inspection notices, and compliance monitoring for unpermitted structures.

Audit Governance

Tamper-proof transaction logging, role-based access controls, and official PDF reporting.
