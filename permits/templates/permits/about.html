eTala

Municipal Engineering Office · Carigara, Leyte

eTala is a web-based engineering records archiving and retrieval platform developed for the Municipal Engineering Office of Carigara, Leyte. It provides authorized personnel with a centralized way to manage engineering records, preserve supporting documents, and quickly locate archived information when needed.

What eTala Does?

Archive Records

Digitally organize and preserve engineering records and supporting documents.

Manage Permits & Projects

Maintain organized information for engineering permits and project records.

Search & Retrieve

Quickly find archived records using relevant record information and filters.

Track Illegal Constructions

Identify, flag, and monitor unpermitted building developments and enforcement compliance.

Monitor Records

View important permit information, including records that require attention.

Track Activities

Maintain a record of important system transactions for accountability.

Engineering Permits

Authorized Users

Engineering Staff
