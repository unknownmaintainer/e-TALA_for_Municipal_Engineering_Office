FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting Guides
Go to the Login screen and click on the 'Forgot Password?' link below the login fields. Enter your registered Carigara LGU staff email address to request a 6-digit OTP reset code. Accounts automatically locked after 10 failed login attempts can be unlocked instantly by a Municipal Admin.
Open any engineering record, navigate to the 'Document Checklist' tab, and click 'Upload Document'. You can drag & drop PDF files or scanned blueprints up to 25MB per document. Authorized staff can also waive optional checklist requirements when allowed.
A special violation flag raised for structures built or started without securing a prior Building Permit (P.D. 1096 Sec. 301). To resolve it, click 'Flag Illegal Structure', select the compliance status (e.g. Regularized / Permit Issued), and attach legal compliance files.
Navigate to the Barangay Workspace or Search module, click 'Map View', and click on any barangay marker pin. You can also view Google Maps coordinates and satellite pins for each project location.
Admins and authorized staff can navigate to 'Activity Logs' or 'Reports' page, filter by date range or barangay, and click 'Export' to download official CSV spreadsheet reports.
Use the persistent Search bar at the top of any page or press Ctrl + K. Type a permit number, applicant name, barangay name, or year to instantly filter results with zero page reload.
